KIFF is a new foundation dedicated to transparent, open-source development. Our branding is currently TBD, but our mission is clear.

What is KIFF?

KIFF is a play on abbreviations and stands for:

  • Keep It Free Foundation
  • Keep It Free Forever
  • Keep it Forever Free
  • Knowledge is Free Forever
  • Keep it Forever Foundation

Our Goals

  • Good Projects: Work on meaningful projects in an open-source, transparent way.
  • Open Source Only: We commit exclusively to open-source projects.
  • OSI Approved: We only allow strictly Open Source Initiative (OSI) approved licenses.
  • Open Governance: Guided by the Open Collective Community Guidelines.
